About

Rokan Uddin Faruqui is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Chittagong. His work spans machine learning and artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and formal methods — including ontology-driven multi-agent systems, model checking, and access control for healthcare workflow systems. He teaches and mentors students across data engineering, data structures and algorithms, database systems, and graduate-level programming languages.
